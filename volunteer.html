Get Involved

Join the Movement

Better Basista is powered and produced by volunteers under BetterGov.PH. Whether you are a developer, designer, researcher, writer, or passionate resident of Pangasinan, your skills can help make local governance more open and accessible to all.

Browse the GitHub org Visit BetterGov.PH
Where You Fit In

No matter your background, there's a role

Developers

Help build and maintain the site, data pipelines, and open-source tools on GitHub.

Designers & Writers

Shape how municipal data reads and looks — from page layouts to plain-language summaries.

Researchers & Residents

Gather and verify public records, ordinances, and budget documents from official sources.

How to Start

Getting involved

Better Basista doesn't have a dedicated contact channel set up yet — as the project grows, this page will list a direct way to reach the local team. For now, the fastest way in is through the wider BetterGov.PH community.

Open an issue or pull request

The site's source is public. If you can see something to fix or add, the GitHub repository is the place to start.

Connect through BetterGov.PH

BetterGov.PH coordinates volunteers across every Better LGU portal, Basista included — reach out through their community channels to get connected.

Better Basista

An independent civic technology initiative for the Municipality of Basista, Pangasinan, built and maintained by volunteers under BetterGov.PH — not an official government website.
