Our Mission

Open Data for a Better Basista

Better Basista is a grassroots civic technology initiative dedicated to bringing data transparency, accessibility, and open public governance tools to the municipality of Basista, Pangasinan. Driven entirely by community volunteers and supported by BetterGov.PH, our initiative empowers local citizens with clear, accessible, and actionable government data.

What We're Building

Three pillars, one goal

Open Access

Making public records, municipal data, and local announcements easy to find and read for all residents.

Civic Tech Tools

Developing open-source digital solutions tailored specifically to the needs of the Basista community.

Volunteer Driven

Uniting developers, designers, researchers, and local advocates to build a more informed community.
