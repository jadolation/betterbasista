Better Basista is an independent, volunteer-led civic technology project operating under the broader umbrella of BetterGov.PH—a volunteer-driven civic technology and open source movement making Philippine government data accessible, understandable, and actionable.

Our Focus

Serving the Municipality of Basista

We focus specifically on localized open-data solutions for Basista. By bridging the gap between raw government reports and citizen awareness, we build tools that strengthen community engagement, promote accountability, and help every Basistanean stay informed.

A 4th class municipality in Pangasinan's 2nd District

Basista sits in Pangasinan's second congressional district, about 29 kilometers from the provincial capital of Lingayen, and is made up of 13 barangays. We're keeping this page intentionally light on statistics for now — official figures for population, land area, and the local budget vary across the sources we've checked so far, and we'd rather publish a verified number later than a convenient-but-inconsistent one today. A full Statistics & Indicators page, modeled on sourced, dated figures with clear provenance, is planned as the project gathers more data.
