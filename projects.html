What's Planned

The kind of tools we're working toward

Officials & Contacts Directory

A clear, sourced directory of Basista's elected and appointed officials, offices, and how to reach them.

Services Guide

Permits, registrations, and frontline services, organized by office, with requirements and processing details.

Budget & Transparency Tracker

Income, expenditure, and infrastructure project data, sourced and dated, so every figure is traceable.
